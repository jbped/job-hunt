# Interview Queue

Questions the agent has asked that you have not answered yet. Leaving one open is
fine — an unanswered question is better than a guessed answer, because everything
here can end up in front of an employer.

Answer them here, or in conversation and let the agent file the answer.

## Open

- What are your target roles beyond your current title?
- Which work model do you want: onsite, hybrid, or remote?
- Are you open to relocation, and within what geography?
- What compensation range are you targeting?
- What do you want to be doing in three years?
