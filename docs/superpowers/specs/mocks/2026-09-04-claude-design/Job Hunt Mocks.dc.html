Senior Frontend Engineer
Globex · Remote (United States) · Engineering · Posted 22 August 2026 · Req GLX-4471 · $180,000–210,000 + equity
Globex is looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to own our design system and raise the bar for the web experience across three product squads. You will define component APIs, lead accessibility work, and mentor engineers who consume the system day to day.
What you'll do
Own the component library end to end, from API design through documentation and adoption. Partner with design on tokens and theming. Drive our accessibility conformance work and hold the line in review. Mentor engineers across squads, including people who don't report to you.
What we're looking for
Five or more years building production front ends, with real ownership of a shared component library. Deep knowledge of TypeScript and modern React. Demonstrated accessibility work beyond automated checks. Evidence of raising the level of engineers around you across team boundaries.
How we hire
A recruiter conversation, a technical screen, then an onsite of four rounds: system design, a frontend deep dive, a values conversation, and a review of work you bring.
